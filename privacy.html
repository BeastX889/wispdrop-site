Wispdrop — Privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026 · applies to Wispdrop for Android and Wispdrop for Mac, published by LumioLab

The short version

Wispdrop moves files and text directly between your own devices on your local network. We don't run any server, we don't have accounts, and nothing you send ever reaches us or anyone else. Wispdrop contains no advertising and no tracking. The one exception to "nothing leaves your devices" is described under QR scanner diagnostics below.

What Wispdrop handles, and where it goes

Files, photos, text and links you sendSent straight from one of your devices to the other over your Wi‑Fi or hotspot, encrypted end to end (X25519 key exchange, AES‑256‑GCM). They are saved only on the receiving device: in Downloads/Wispdrop on the Mac; in Pictures/Wispdrop, Movies/Wispdrop or Download/Wispdrop on Android. Text you receive is placed on that device's clipboard.
Device nameYour device's name (for example "Galaxy S23 Ultra") is announced on your local network so your other device can find it, the same way AirPrint or Chromecast devices announce themselves. You can change it in Wispdrop's settings.
Device key and paired devicesEach install creates its own encryption key and remembers the devices you paired with and their last local address. This stays in Wispdrop's private storage on that device and is never sent anywhere except the public part of the key, which your other device uses to recognise it.
Camera (Android)Used only while you scan a pairing QR code shown on your Mac. Camera frames are processed on the phone to read the code and are never stored or sent anywhere.
Recent activityThe list of recent transfers is kept in memory while the app runs and is cleared when it closes.

What we collect

Nothing. LumioLab receives no personal data, usage data, crash reports, identifiers or file contents from Wispdrop. Wispdrop's own code never contacts the internet; it talks only to your other device on your local network.

QR scanner diagnostics (Android only)

To read pairing QR codes, Wispdrop for Android uses Google's ML Kit barcode scanner, which runs on the phone. Google's ML Kit library may send Google limited diagnostic data about how the scanner performs: device model and Android version, Wispdrop's package name and version, a per‑installation identifier, and scanner performance and error metrics. It does not include camera images, the codes you scan, or anything you send with Wispdrop. Google states this data is used for diagnostics and usage analytics, is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure. LumioLab never receives this data. Wispdrop for Mac contains no such component.

Sharing with third parties

Wispdrop shares nothing with anyone. Apart from the ML Kit diagnostics above, which Google collects for its own library, Wispdrop includes no third‑party analytics, advertising or tracking libraries.

Your control

  • Forget a paired device at any time in Wispdrop's settings on either device.
  • Uninstalling Wispdrop, or clearing its data, deletes its key, settings and paired devices. Files you received stay where they were saved until you delete them.

Children

Wispdrop is a general‑purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.
